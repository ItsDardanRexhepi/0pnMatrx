# Redline for counsel — the platform rename

**Status: NOT APPLIED.** `web/terms.html` and `web/privacy.html` are unchanged.
This file records the edits the rename would make to them and the one question
that is not mine to answer. Nothing here is legal advice.

The platform formerly called **0pnMatrx** (also written OpenMatrix) is now
**The Matrix**. The rename has been applied throughout the software. It has
deliberately NOT been applied to the two published documents below, because they
are a live agreement with users and one clause turns on a trademark question.

## The question that blocks the rest

`terms.html`, Trademarks, currently reads:

> The names "0pnMatrx," "OpenMatrix," "MTRX," "Glasswing," "NeoSafe," and
> associated logos and visual identities are trademarks of the Operator.

A mechanical rename turns that into a claim of trademark over **"The Matrix"**
and **"Matrix"**. Two things make that worth a lawyer's eye before it is
published:

1. *The Matrix* is a well-known film property with active registrations in
   entertainment and software classes. Asserting it as the Operator's mark in a
   published contract is a different act from using it as a product name.
2. The former names were coined and therefore distinctive. "Matrix" is a common
   English word used by other products. Whatever protection the old clause
   asserted may not carry over.

Please advise on the wording of this clause. The remaining edits are
straightforward substitutions and are listed below for completeness.

## Two facts the substitutions depend on

* **The repository URL changes only when the repository is renamed.** Until
  `github.com/ItsDardanRexhepi/TheMatrix` is renamed to `the-matrix`, the current
  links are the ones that resolve and the proposed ones 404. Apply the URL rows
  after the rename, not before.
* **`openmatrix-ai.com` is unchanged throughout.** Both documents name it as the
  hosted service. A domain is only a name if it is owned, so it keeps the old
  name until a domain under the new one is acquired and serving.
